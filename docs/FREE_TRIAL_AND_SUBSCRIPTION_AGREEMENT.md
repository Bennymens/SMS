## 2. Free Trial

### 2.1 Eligibility
Each Subscriber organisation is entitled to **one (1) Free Trial** of the Service. The Free Trial is non-transferable and may not be claimed more than once per institution, email domain, or beneficial owner.

### 2.2 Duration
The Free Trial Period commences on the date the Subscriber's account is activated and lasts for **fourteen (14) calendar days**.

### 2.3 What Is Included During the Free Trial
During the Free Trial Period, the Subscriber shall have access to the full feature set of the selected Subscription Plan (Starter, Growth, or Enterprise). Specific limits applicable to the selected plan (student count, storage, number of Authorised Users) remain in force during the Free Trial.

### 2.4 No Credit Card Required
No payment information is required to activate the Free Trial. The Company will not charge the Subscriber during the Free Trial Period.

### 2.5 Conversion to Paid Subscription
At the end of the Free Trial Period, the Subscriber may:
- (a) Elect to subscribe to a paid Subscription Plan, at which point this Agreement continues in full force; or  
- (b) Allow the trial to expire without subscribing, at which point access to the Service will be suspended.

The Company may send reminder notifications at **Day 10** and **Day 13** of the Free Trial Period inviting the Subscriber to upgrade.

### 2.6 Data During and After the Free Trial
All School Data created during the Free Trial Period will be retained for **thirty (30) days** after trial expiry. If the Subscriber converts to a paid plan within that 30-day window, the data will be fully accessible. After 30 days without conversion, School Data may be permanently deleted.

### 2.7 No Obligation
The Free Trial is provided "as-is". The Company makes no warranties specific to the Free Trial beyond those set out in Section 10.

## 4. Billing & Payment Terms

### 4.1 Billing Cycle
- **Monthly plans**: Billed on the same calendar date each month corresponding to the date the paid subscription commenced.
- **Annual plans**: Billed as a single up-front payment on commencement, and on each annual anniversary thereof.

### 4.2 Accepted Payment Methods
The Company accepts payment via:
- MTN Mobile Money (MoMo) and Telecel Cash
- Visa, Mastercard, and Verve debit/credit cards (via Stripe or Paystack)
- Direct bank transfer (Stanbic Bank, Ecobank virtual account)
- Cheque (annual plans only, subject to clearance before activation)

### 4.3 Invoices
The Company shall issue a VAT-compliant invoice to the Subscriber's registered billing email address on or before the due date of each payment.

### 4.4 Late Payments
If a payment is not received within **fourteen (14) days** of the invoice due date:
- (a) The Company may send an overdue notice and a 7-day cure period.
- (b) If payment is not received within the cure period, the Company may suspend access to the Service without prejudice to its right to recover outstanding amounts.
- (c) A late payment fee of **5% of the outstanding invoice value** may be applied for each 30-day period the payment remains outstanding.

### 4.5 Taxes
All Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes, levies, and duties, including Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) Value Added Tax (VAT) at the prevailing statutory rate. The Subscriber is responsible for all applicable taxes in its jurisdiction.

### 4.6 Disputed Invoices
The Subscriber must notify the Company of any bona fide billing dispute within **14 days** of receiving the invoice. Undisputed amounts remain payable on schedule.

## 5. Auto-Renewal & Cancellation

### 5.1 Auto-Renewal
Subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each Subscription Period (monthly or annual) at the then-current plan rate, unless cancelled in accordance with this Section.

### 5.2 Cancellation by the Subscriber
- The Subscriber may cancel at any time through the Admin Portal under **Settings → Subscription → Cancel Plan**, or by written notice to the Company.
- **Monthly plans**: Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current monthly billing period. No refund is issued for unused days within a paid month.
- **Annual plans**: Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current annual term. No pro-rated refund is issued for the unused portion of a prepaid annual period, except where expressly required by applicable law.

### 5.3 Cancellation Confirmation
Upon cancellation, the Company will send a written confirmation to the Subscriber's registered email address within **2 business days**.

### 5.4 Effect of Cancellation
On the effective date of cancellation:
- Access to the Service will be suspended.
- School Data will be retained for **60 days** to allow the Subscriber to export data (see Section 16).
- After 60 days, School Data will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from the Company's systems.

## 6. Plan Changes & Upgrades

### 6.1 Upgrading
- Upgrades take effect immediately.
- For monthly subscribers, the price difference for the remainder of the current billing month is charged on a **pro-rated basis** at the time of upgrade.
- For annual subscribers, a pro-rated credit from the current plan and a new annual charge for the upgraded plan are applied.

### 6.2 Downgrading
- Downgrade requests take effect at the **start of the next Subscription Period**.
- If the Subscriber's current data or user counts exceed the limits of the downgraded plan, the Subscriber must reduce usage before the downgrade date or the request will not be processed.

### 6.3 Limit Warnings
The Company will notify the Subscriber by email and in-platform notification when the Subscriber reaches **90%** of any plan limit (students, storage, Authorised Users). The Company will never suspend access without providing at least **30 days' advance notice** of a potential limit breach.

## 9. Data Security

### 9.1 Security Measures
The Company implements the following security controls:
- **Encryption at rest**: AES-256 encryption for all stored School Data.
- **Encryption in transit**: TLS 1.3 for all data transmitted to and from the Service.
- **Authentication**: Role-based access control (RBAC) with unique credentials per Authorised User.
- **Backups**: Automated daily encrypted backups with a **30-day retention window**.
- **Monitoring**: 24/7 infrastructure monitoring and intrusion detection.

### 9.2 Security Incidents
In the event of a confirmed personal data breach affecting School Data, the Company will:
- (a) Notify the Subscriber within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach.
- (b) Provide details of the nature of the breach, categories of data affected, and remediation steps taken.
- (c) Cooperate with the Subscriber and relevant authorities in the investigation.

### 9.3 Subscriber Responsibilities
The Subscriber is responsible for:
- (a) The security and confidentiality of Authorised User credentials.
- (b) Ensuring School Data uploaded to the Service does not itself contain embedded malware or violate any third-party rights.

## 10. Service Availability & SLA

### 10.1 Uptime Commitment
The Company targets a monthly uptime of **99.9%** for the core Service (excluding scheduled maintenance windows). Uptime is measured on a calendar-month basis.

### 10.2 Scheduled Maintenance
Scheduled maintenance windows will be communicated to the Subscriber at least **48 hours in advance** via in-platform notice and/or email. Maintenance will be scheduled outside standard Ghanaian school operating hours where reasonably practicable.

### 10.3 SLA Credits

| Uptime Achieved | Credit |
|---|---|
| 99.0% – 99.89% | 5% of monthly fee |
| 95.0% – 98.99% | 10% of monthly fee |
| Below 95.0% | 20% of monthly fee |

Credits must be requested within **30 days** of the affected month and will be applied to the next invoice. Credits are the Subscriber's sole remedy for downtime.

### 10.4 Exclusions
Uptime SLA does not apply to unavailability caused by:
- (a) The Subscriber's own infrastructure, internet connectivity, or devices.
- (b) Third-party payment gateway outages (MTN MoMo, Paystack, Stripe, etc.).
- (c) Force majeure events as defined in Section 13.4.
- (d) Actions taken by the Subscriber or Authorised Users.

## 16. Data Export & Return After Termination

### 16.1 Export Window
For **60 days** following the expiry or termination of this Agreement (the "Export Window"), the Company will make the Service available to the Subscriber in a read-only capacity solely to allow the Subscriber to export School Data.

### 16.2 Export Formats
School Data may be exported in the following formats:
- CSV (student records, financial ledgers, attendance logs, grades)
- PDF (report cards, invoices, receipts)

### 16.3 Deletion
At the end of the Export Window, the Company will **permanently delete** all School Data from its active systems and backups, unless earlier deletion is requested by the Subscriber in writing.

### 16.4 Deletion Certificate
Upon request, the Company will provide a written certification confirming permanent deletion of School Data within **15 business days** of deletion completion.
